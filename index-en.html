The Game of Go

Go, known as Baduk in Korean, is one of the oldest board games in the world. With over 2,500 years of history, Go is a strategy game that combines simple rules with infinite strategic complexity.

Played on a 19×19 board, two players place black and white stones alternately, competing to control more territory. It is a game that challenges logic, intuition, and strategic vision.
